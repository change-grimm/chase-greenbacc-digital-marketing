Position description

Digital Marketing & Platform Testing Associate

As online marketing becomes more data-driven, businesses need reliable ways to test digital experiences and evaluate campaign performance. This role supports approved marketing activities by checking customer journeys, validating analytics, and carrying out quality-assurance tasks on designated platforms.

The associate follows documented test plans and platform policies. Testing activity is identified as testing where appropriate, and reporting distinguishes test traffic from real customer activity. The goal is to improve data quality and marketing operations—not to manufacture engagement, impersonate customers, or bypass bot-detection systems.

Transparency: Legitimate testing should use authorized accounts, approved devices and networks, and clearly designated test transactions when required. Never pay money to “unlock” work, route funds for an unknown party, or share passwords, one-time codes, or banking access.
